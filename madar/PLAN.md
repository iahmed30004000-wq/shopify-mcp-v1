One source of truth for everything that is left: the owner's original brief, every
decision he has made since, his feedback on APK #15, and his new ideas.
`.resume/RESUME.md` keeps only the live "where are we right now" state and the raw
feedback; **this file is the ordered plan**.

**Working rule (owner, standing):** one part at a time → finish it → verify it →
ship an APK → report in plain Arabic with an example → then the next part.
At most 1–2 agents at a time. No duplicate verification passes when CI runs the
same checks. Never leave several half-finished things running.

**Religious content (owner, standing, 2026-10-16):** every hadith and every dhikr in the app
carries **its source and its grading** (sahih / hasan / da'if, named) - not a bare text. Nothing
religious is generated by a model, anywhere. The **kids section's religious material does not
ship until the sheikh the owner names approves it**: it is built and kept **hidden behind a flag**
until that name arrives, so the work is done but a child cannot reach it. Where the schools of law
differ, the app **says it is a difference** and gives the positions - never one ruling as if it
were the only one.

**Shipped so far:** APK #15 (commit d9d5204) = foundation + Astrolabe Orbit + Faith
(prayer, adhan, tracker, adhkar, Quran, recitation, wird, Hifz, qibla) + Health +
Money + the six Life planets + Madar Cinema hall (demo, Metropolis Machine,

**Built and verified but NOT yet reachable in the app:** global search, notification
centre, data export + encrypted backup, AI chat (own key), home-screen widgets,
Together Mode (core, couple specials, Nearby + optional online transports), and the
rules engines for ~45 Tier 2 games.

## Part 3 — The AI bridge: copy/share JSON round trip  → APK #18

Decided by the owner (2026-10-07): **no login**, no mandatory API key. Madar talks to any
AI app (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude) by handing over a file and taking one back.

1. **Export**: he picks what to send (reuse the data package's per-section chooser); Madar
   produces one JSON **plus a ready prompt** that tells the AI what to analyse and the exact
   answer shape. Share sheet or copy.
2. **Answer**: a "Madar plan" JSON we define – suggested tasks, habits, routines, meal-plan
   changes, budget tweaks, learning programs, reminders, notes; every item carries its reason.
3. **Import**: share/paste back → validate → show every suggestion as a card with its reason →
   he approves / edits / rejects each one → only approved items are applied. Nothing silent.
4. **The approved items become a checkable list** (owner, 2026-10-10, option A of two he
   picked): the items he approves gather under one named plan - his own name, editable - and
   he ticks them off one by one and sees «٢ من ٤». A ticked item is a record of an approved
   suggestion, **not** a row in the home panel's task list; "send this to my tasks" may come
   later as its own action. Example he gave: he sends a month of data, gets 7 suggestions,
   approves 4, and has «خطة تشرين الأول» with ☐ walk 20 minutes after dinner · ☐ check the
   electricity bill · ☐ log your food 5 days running · ☐ call your mother on Friday.
5. **History**: keep applied plans and later show before/after from his own data.
6. Health suggestions stay "track & practise", never diagnosis or treatment.
7. The same import pipe carries the learning-planet JSON (Part 6) and kitchen data (Part 5).
8. The API-key chat stays as an optional shortcut for the same round trip.

## Part 9b — «تذكير الصيام»: the voluntary fasts  → its own APK

Owner, 2026-10-16, inside the Faith planet. **Everything here repeats by itself, every month and
every year** - he sets it once and never touches it again.

**What is reminded**
- **Weekly**: Monday and Thursday.
- **Monthly**: the white days - **13, 14 and 15 of the Hijri month**.
- **Yearly**: **Arafah**; **Tasu'a and Ashura**; **the six of Shawwal**; **the first ten of Dhu
  al-Hijjah**; and **more fasting through Muharram and Sha'ban** (a gentle in-month nudge, not a
  day-by-day alarm).

**When**: **the night before, after Isha** - taken from the prayer schedule already in the app,
not a fixed clock time. The notification carries two actions: **«ناوي»** and **«لأ»**, and
«ناوي» writes the intention so the morning has it. **A log of the days he fasted** is kept and
shown in the Faith planet.

**Where there must be no reminder at all**: **the two Eids** and **the days of Tashriq** (11, 12
and 13 Dhu al-Hijjah) - fasting them is forbidden. **13 Dhu al-Hijjah is therefore dropped from
the white days that month**, and that month's white days are only 14 and 15. **In Ramadan there
is no Monday/Thursday reminder** (the obligation covers the whole month).

**The Hijri risk, and his fix**: a calculated Hijri date can be **a day off from Jordan's
announcement**. ⚠️ noted, 🔧 **a setting that shifts the Hijri date one day earlier or later**,
and every rule above - the white days, Arafah, Ashura, the ten, Tashriq, the Eids - follows that
shift, not just the display.

**DoD — works**: each of the weekly, monthly and yearly reminders fires on its own correct night
after Isha, with no reminder on an Eid or a day of Tashriq; 13 Dhu al-Hijjah never appears as a
white day; no Monday/Thursday reminder lands inside Ramadan; «ناوي» records the intention and
«لأ» silences only that day; the fasting log shows the month and the year and counts the six of
Shawwal and the white days separately; the ±1 day setting moves every rule; all of it re-arms
itself at the start of each Hijri month and each year without him opening the app.
**DoD — tests**: a pure-rules suite over **a full Hijri year** that asserts the exact set of
reminded days, including the three exclusions, with the offset at -1, 0 and +1; the schedule
re-arming across a month boundary and a year boundary; the two actions writing exactly one row
each and nothing when the app is dead (the meds background isolate is the pattern); the
notification ids inside their own block; `NotificationGate` holding them when the Faith namespace
is muted; restore re-arming them; a Ramadan month asserted to contain no Monday/Thursday
reminder.
**DoD — edge cases**: Isha that does not occur at a high latitude (the schedule's existing
fallback); a traveller crossing time zones on the night before Arafah; a Hijri month of 29 days;
the offset changed while a reminder is already pending; the user already fasting a day he was not
reminded about (he can log it by hand); battery saver and Doze.
**DoD — blockers**: none - the prayer schedule, the Hijri calendar, the notification gate and the
tracker all exist and are tested. The **grading and source rule** above applies to every dhikr or
hadith shown with a fast.
