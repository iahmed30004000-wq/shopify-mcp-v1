Madar – adhkar content credits (assets/adhkar/hisn_al_muslim.json)
=====================================================================

The adhkar are from "Hisn al-Muslim" (Fortress of the Muslim) by Sa'id ibn
'Ali ibn Wahf al-Qahtani: supplications and remembrances from the Quran and
the Sunnah, with their hadith references. Madar bundles the morning,
evening, after-prayer, before-sleep and on-waking sets (79 entries).

The text was taken from openly licensed datasets, corrected and
normalised (see assets/adhkar/source/build_adhkar.py – every change is
listed there – and assets/adhkar/source/verification_log.txt):

1. Morning and evening adhkar (Arabic text, repetition counts, Arabic
   virtues and the hadith references)
   Seen-Arabic/Morning-And-Evening-Adhkar-DB
   https://github.com/Seen-Arabic/Morning-And-Evening-Adhkar-DB
   commit 29d7623fede52eca835a789025dfda866e8cfe44 – MIT License (below)

2. After-prayer, before-sleep and on-waking adhkar (Arabic text and
   counts)
   YousefAsalya/Islamic-Pro-azkar-API (data/ar.json)
   https://github.com/YousefAsalya/Islamic-Pro-azkar-API
   commit d793023e3b69c91674023f692425950c8ebbb64d – MIT License (below)
   The dataset states it was collected from hisnmuslim.com. Its audio
   files are NOT bundled (their licence is not stated), nor is its English.

3. Quran verses quoted in the adhkar (Ayat al-Kursi, al-Baqarah 285-286,
   Al Imran 190-200, al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, an-Nas): the fully vowelled
   imla'i text "ara-quranspelled" (itself from Arabic Wikisource), cross-
   checked word by word against "ara-quransimple" (Tanzil "simple", via
   alquran.cloud)
   fawazahmed0/quran-api
   https://github.com/fawazahmed0/quran-api
   commit 47ca096b0976443ba2eab2e45cdf0fb4096a2610 – The Unlicense (public
   domain dedication, below)

English meanings and English virtues were written for Madar from the
Arabic. The two datasets' own English is NOT used: their README (Seen-
Arabic) and fetch script (Islamic-Pro) show it was copied from
hisnmuslim.com and sunnah.com, whose translations carry no open licence
of their own – an MIT file on a copy cannot license someone else's
translation. The English references are the datasets' bibliographic
citations (collection, volume, page, hadith number).

Hadith references for the after-prayer, sleep and waking sets were written
out by Madar in the standard numbering (al-Bukhari / Muslim: Fath al-Bari
and Fu'ad 'Abd al-Baqi numbering); the collections cited match the
footnotes of an older Hisn al-Muslim edition (which cites volume/page). Short notes and a few virtues were worded by
Madar from the hadith. English translations of Quran verses are NOT
bundled (the datasets carried published Quran translations whose own
licences are not open), so Quran-only entries show the Arabic in both
languages with the surah and ayah numbers.

Verification only (never bundled): rn0x/hisn_almuslim_json (older edition,
independent typing; no licence file), fitrahive/dua-dhikr (MIT, an
independent compilation), osamayy/azkar-db (no licence file).

Tasbeeh phrases are single well-known words of remembrance (public domain);
their English meanings were written for Madar.

Audio: Madar bundles no recordings. Users may attach their own recordings
per dhikr; Madar never uses generated voices for Quran or adhkar.
