Madar Cinema – word & knowledge game content credits (assets/games/)
======================================================================

Word Guess, Word Search, Crossword, Islamic Quiz, Capitals & Flags and the
Typing Race use the files in assets/games/. They are rebuilt reproducibly
by assets/games/source/build_games_words.py from pinned sources (downloaded
from raw.githubusercontent.com at the commits below and checked against
their SHA-256); every check is recorded in
assets/games/source/verification_log.txt. Retrieved 2026-09-30.

4. Quran text – referenced, never copied
----------------------------------------
Quiz citations are verified against, and Typing Race Quran passages are read
verbatim from, the bundled Tanzil Uthmani text and metadata
(assets/quran/quran-uthmani.txt, assets/quran/quran-meta.json; Tanzil
Project, CC BY 3.0 with Tanzil's terms – see assets/licenses/quran_credits.txt).
The games never alter or re-store the Quran text: typing_passages.json holds
references (sura, ayah range) only.


5. Madar's own content (self-authored for Madar)
-------------------------------------------------
- Vowelled forms and curation of the Word Guess answers (word_guess_answers.json)
  and of every curated lexicon entry;
- Word Search themes (word_search_themes.json);
- Crossword clues (crossword_clues.json) – original Arabic clues;
- Islamic Quiz (islamic_quiz.json) – original Arabic questions with English
  translations; the cited Quran ayat and hadith are the sources of the facts,
  the wording of every question and option is Madar's;
- Countries, capitals and code-drawn flag specifications (countries.json) –
  compiled for Madar; national flags are public symbols, drawn here from
  geometric descriptions (no images are included);
- Typing Race sentences and passages (typing_passages.json, kinds "original"
  and "vowelled").
Licence: part of Madar, under the same terms as the app.


6. Proverbs and verse (typing_passages.json, kind "saying")
-----------------------------------------------------------
Traditional Arabic proverbs (anonymous folk sayings, public domain) and
single verses by poets who died long ago – al-Mutanabbi (d. 965), Ahmad
Shawqi (d. 1932), Hafiz Ibrahim (d. 1932) and Abu al-Qasim al-Shabbi
(d. 1934) – whose works are in the public domain.
