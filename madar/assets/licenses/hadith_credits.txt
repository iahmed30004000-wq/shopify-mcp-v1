Madar – hadith content credits (assets/hadith/nawawi40.json)
============================================================

An-Nawawi's Forty Hadith (الأربعون النووية), compiled by Imam Yahya ibn
Sharaf an-Nawawi (d. 676 AH / 1277 CE): 42 hadith, Arabic text with the
takhrij (who recorded each hadith and its grading). The Arabic text of the
collection is a classical work in the public domain.

What Madar changed (display clean-up only; no word of a narration was
altered):
  - removed HTML line breaks, stray quotation marks, directional
    formatting characters and bracketed page / number references
    (e.g. "[رقم: 2318]", "2/246");
  - joined "و سلم" into "وسلم", tidied spaces around punctuation;
  - split the takhrij ("رواه البخاري ومسلم" …) into its own field; for
    hadith 5 and 19 the additional narration quoted by an-Nawawi ("وفي
    رواية لمسلم …", "وفي رواية غير الترمذي …") stays with the text;
  - removed one duplicated takhrij (hadith 13).
  The short Arabic and English titles of each hadith were written for
  Madar (they are descriptive labels, not translations).

English translation: NOT bundled. The dataset also offers an English
edition (eng-nawawi) but names no translator or source, so its licence
cannot be confirmed as open; Madar shows the Arabic text in both
languages, with English titles only.
