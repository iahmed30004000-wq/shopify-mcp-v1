Madar offline city list (assets/geo/cities.json)
================================================

The offline city list used for prayer times (names in Arabic and English,
coordinates, IANA time zones and countries) was compiled for Madar from the
openly licensed sources below. Arabic names of Arab-world cities were
written and checked by hand; other names come from the sources and were
corrected by hand where needed. Coordinates and time zones were checked and
corrected (see test/features/prayer/geo_tool/build_cities.py in the source
repository for the exact procedure).

1. Natural Earth – 1:10m Cultural Vectors, Populated Places (v5.x)
   Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.
   Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_10m_populated_places.geojson
   Repository commit: ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19
   Licence: public domain ("All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map
   data found on this website are in the public domain."
   https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)
   Used for: coordinates, populations, English/Arabic names of most cities.

2. GeoNames – cities with a population > 1000, via the "cities.json" dataset
   by lutangar.
   Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lutangar/cities.json/master/cities.json
   Repository commit: e66576247c5d26b1fc65d96a499acff3c3fef827
   Data: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), licensed under the Creative
   Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0,
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
   Used for: coordinates of smaller cities not in Natural Earth (for example
   Madaba, Jerash, Ajloun, Bethlehem, Jenin, Tetouan, Sharm El Sheikh).
   Changes: selected entries only; names replaced by Madar's Arabic and
   English names; coordinates rounded to 4 decimals.

3. IANA Time Zone Database – zone.tab
   Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eggert/tz/main/zone.tab
   Repository commit: 924aa6c5632d7137402b2bbbd0ccb447c9ebb3ac
   Licence: public domain.
   Used for: validating and assigning each city's IANA time zone.

4. Unicode CLDR – territory display names (Arabic and English)
   Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unicode-org/cldr/main/common/main/ar.xml
           https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unicode-org/cldr/main/common/main/en.xml
   Repository commit: a2b41d52212a9bc7744503f04a816a33dd96fc6e
   Licence: Unicode License v3 (SPDX: Unicode-3.0), reproduced below.
   Used for: country names (a few adjusted to their everyday form).
