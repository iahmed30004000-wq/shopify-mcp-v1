Madar adhan and alert sounds
============================

1. Madar tanbih tones (android/app/src/main/res/raw/madar_tanbih_*.wav)
   "Dawn light", "Astrolabe brass", "Serenity", the reminder chime and the
   sunrise crystal are Madar's own work: synthesised procedurally in pure
   Dart (struck-bowl, bell, glass and crystal resonator models, a Freeverb-
   style room and a soft limiter) by lib/features/adhan/sound/tanbih_synth.dart
   on top of Madar's synthesiser (lib/core/sound/synth/). No recording,
   sample or third-party sound is used; the files are regenerated byte for
   byte by `dart run tool/generate_adhan_tones.dart`. They are alert tones –
   never a human voice and never a melody imitating the adhan.
   Licence: part of Madar, under the same terms as the app.

2. No human adhan recordings are bundled.
   Before shipping, GitHub was searched (28 Sep 2026) for adhan / azan /
   athan recordings with an explicit open licence (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA,
   public domain) and verifiable provenance, e.g.:
     - search "adhan mp3" → github.com/Kiwifu/adhan-mp3 ("free for Islamic
       apps", no LICENSE file; recordings of named, commercially published
       muezzins – provenance not verifiable) – rejected;
     - github.com/abodehq/Athan-MP3 – no licence statement – rejected;
     - searches with license:cc0-1.0 / cc-by-4.0 / cc-by-sa-4.0 / unlicense
       found only software repositories (github.com/Mahdiisdumb/Dramatic-Ahdan,
       github.com/QuanticX/The5PortableAdhan), no recording with a
       verifiable licence of its own – rejected.
   Wikimedia Commons and archive.org were not reachable from the build
   machine. Users may attach recordings they own or are licensed to use;
   those files stay on their device and are never distributed with Madar.

3. The supplication after the adhan (shown on the adhan screen)
   Text of the hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari, no. 614 (Book of the Adhan),
   narrated by Jabir ibn Abdullah – classical Arabic text in the public
   domain. The English rendering in the app is Madar's own translation.
